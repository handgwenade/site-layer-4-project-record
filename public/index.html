Site Layer 4: what's proposed near Wheatland

The applicant proposes a data-center and energy campus with gas generation, solar power, and battery storage. This independent public record helps residents follow decisions, understand proposed safeguards, and find opportunities to comment.

We distinguish documented decisions, applicant statements, proposed protections, and unanswered questions.

Where things stand

Zoning approval has been reported. The county reportedly approved industrial zoning on September 2. The final signed conditions remain unverified. Rezoning alone does not authorize construction of the campus.

Countywide rules are still proposed. Planning & Zoning recommended changes for data centers, expansion, and associated power facilities. Final adoption has not been verified.

The state review path remains unresolved. Reviewed records show preliminary coordination, but do not establish the project's permitting or exemption outcome.

Federal tariff accepted with a compliance condition. FERC accepted Tri-State's large-load tariff subject to a compliance condition. Tri-State filed its response September 9; acceptance of that response remains unverified.
