What to actually say

For county-rule comments, pick one or two issues below. Cite the relevant rule or record, explain your concern and state the change you want. These are proposed requests, not adopted requirements; some build on existing rules, and authority must be established. Federal comments should address the narrower filing question above.

  • Water: Ask for a defined water budget, independent baseline, nearby-well monitoring and lawful, funded mitigation. Request reconciliation of the applicant’s differing estimates.
  • Enforcement: Ask who inspects, how often, what gets reported publicly and which authorized remedies apply when conditions are breached.
  • Decommissioning: Ask for funded, drawable security tied to teardown and restoration costs. Zoning reversion is not a cleanup fund.
  • Who pays / public costs: Ask who funds roads, emergency services and independent review, and when commitments become binding. Independent-review escrow authority remains unverified.
  • Operating standards: Ask for measurable noise and lighting limits, appropriate setbacks and a clear compliance process.
  • State oversight: Ask which state-review route applies, what protections it supplies and which gaps the county can lawfully address.

Make it specific. Cite a record, describe relevant experience and state a clear request.

Paper filing instructions

Paper alternative: Address filings to the Secretary, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission. USPS mail goes to 888 First Street NE, Washington, DC 20426. Hand delivery and other carriers go to 12225 Wilkins Avenue, Rockville, MD 20852. Allow for receipt and processing; do not rely on the postmark. Consult current copy and service requirements for the document being filed. FERC delivery guidance ↗ (opens in a new tab); Rule 2001 ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Party status and intervention

A comment or protest does not make you a party. Intervention is a separate filing with interest, timing, and service requirements; late intervention is not assured. A protest must be served on the person against whom it is directed. Intervenors also have service obligations. Follow the intervention guidance ↗ (opens in a new tab) and applicable Rules 211, 214 and 2010 ↗ (opens in a new tab). Do not assume this compliance notice reopens every issue decided in August.

Help: FERC’s Office of Public Participation: 202-502-6595, OPP@ferc.gov. These are assistance contacts; an ordinary email is not a docket filing. (E5 ↗ (opens in a new tab), E6 ↗ (opens in a new tab))
