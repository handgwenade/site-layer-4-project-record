Participation · county and federal decisions

Take action: use the process that addresses your concern

County land-use rules and the federal utility compliance filing address different decisions. Identify the proposal or document, explain the concern, and state the change you are requesting. The safeguard requests below are proposals; some build on existing rules or meeting discussion, and others are new requests.

County: proposed land-use rules

Public hearing: October 7, 2026, at 1 p.m. Mountain Daylight Time, in the Commissioner Room, County Courthouse, 800 9th Street, Wheatland. The Board of County Commissioners will hear comments on proposed county land-use rule changes. This is not an advertised Site Layer 4 SUP hearing, and the notice does not guarantee a vote that day. The county gives the local time; MDT identifies the applicable local timezone. Official notice

Written comments: Email planner@plattecountywyoming.com, or deliver them to the Planning Office in the courthouse. The notice states no separate written-comment cutoff. Sending comments early is practical advice, not an official deadline. Keep a copy and seek receipt confirmation if needed; this site cannot promise acknowledgment or a particular records-retention outcome. C6

Read the proposed amendments. Identify the chapter or section and distinguish a requested countywide standard from a future project-specific condition. For example (proposed comment wording):

Please retain review of expansion beyond the original SUP’s approved server capacity, power draw, or cooling infrastructure, and separate review of associated generation and storage. Please also identify which lawful instruments will establish measurable operating limits, funded public-service obligations, and a project inspection and reporting program. Existing general enforcement rules should be carried through into clear project responsibilities.

For water concerns, refer to the unreconciled applicant estimates and request a defined water budget, independent baseline, monitoring, and lawful secured mitigation. For cleanup, distinguish zoning reversion from an actual drawable security instrument. See P1–P8 for specific proposed safeguards. These requests do not establish that every condition is within county authority. (C4; COUNTY-008–009,021,023,033; WATER-001–015)

Electronic route: use eFiling. eComment does not accept this ER docket. Follow FERC’s official eFiling instructions:

  1. Prepare a signed comment identifying ER26-1929-002 and the September 9 compliance filing.
  2. Register and validate an account at FERC Online, then select eFiling.
  3. Select General → Comment (on Filing, Environ. Report, or Tech Conf) and choose the exact subdocket.
  4. Upload the public document, identify the filing party and signer/contact, review the summary, and submit.
  5. Retain confirmation and check the acceptance/rejection notice and docket entry. A saved draft is not a completed filing.

An example of a comment relevant to this filing is:

Please determine whether revised §§3.6 and 5.3.1 fully implement the August order’s requirement to file non-conforming agreements containing differing Basin terms. Please identify how the filing commitment and its evaluation-based timing apply to those agreements.

A comment or protest does not make you a party. Intervention is a separate filing with interest, timing, and service requirements; late intervention is not assured. A protest must be served on the person against whom it is directed. Intervenors also have service obligations. Follow the intervention guidance and applicable Rules 211, 214 and 2010. Do not assume this compliance notice reopens every issue decided in August.

Paper alternative: Address filings to the Secretary, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission. USPS mail goes to 888 First Street NE, Washington, DC 20426. Hand delivery and other carriers go to 12225 Wilkins Avenue, Rockville, MD 20852. Allow for receipt and processing; do not rely on the postmark. Consult current copy and service requirements for the document being filed. FERC delivery guidance; Rule 2001

Help: FERC’s Office of Public Participation: 202-502-6595, OPP@ferc.gov. These are assistance contacts; an ordinary email is not a docket filing. (E5, E6)
