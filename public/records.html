Records behind this site

Project decisions and filings: County application; signed August P&Z minutes; posted rule proposal; official hearing notice; federal order and compliance package. Secondary reporting is distinguished from adopted instruments.

Project correspondence and statements: DEQ release and dated status reproduction; county planner reports and response provenance; Wheatland REA’s member letter. Statements by the applicant, consultant or utility retain their attribution.

Current legal framework: Current county rules and separate solar chapter; current state statutes; FERC tariff and filing guidance. Check applicability before treating a general rule as a project obligation.

Comparison records and research leads: BFC/Cheyenne exemption records, Chugwater material, air-permit inventories and unfinished searches are available in Project notes. Comparison records do not determine Site Layer 4’s status.

How to read this site: A direct record supports what that document says. An applicant statement is not an independent technical finding. Analysis explains an inference. A proposed request is not adopted law. “Unresolved” identifies the limits of the reviewed evidence.

Privacy and fairness: Summaries omit unnecessary personal contact details. Original public records may contain them. Absence from a partial production is not proof of wrongdoing. Corrections, original records and dated search boundaries remain available.
