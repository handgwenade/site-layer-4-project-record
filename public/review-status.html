About the record and evidence limits

This independent public-records resource follows the proposed Site Layer 4 data-center and energy campus near Wheatland. It distinguishes agency actions, applicant statements, general rules, project commitments and proposed safeguards.

Who maintains this record

This site is maintained independently by a Platte County resident using publicly available records. It is not an official county website and is not affiliated with the project applicant.

Its purpose is to distinguish documented facts, applicant statements, proposed protections, unresolved questions and opportunities for public participation.

How to read the evidence

“Not established in the reviewed records” does not mean “does not exist.” A staff report is not a signed final decision. A general rule is not proof of a project's executed agreement or posted security. A proposed safeguard is not an adopted requirement.

The detailed evidence checks retain their September 20, 2026 review dates. Participation notices and the bounded federal docket search were rechecked September 21; the latest update below states exactly what was checked. Historical observations retain their original dates.

Original records and limited excerpts

The downloadable agency email PDFs and their ZIP are preserved as received. Print headers, footers and embedded links are part of those copies; an embedded mailbox or meeting link is not this site's public source-access route. Use the download links on the Records page.

The C2, C3 and C9 county passages are limited research transcriptions with provenance, scope, qualifications and omission notices, not complete or county-hosted originals. The full C9 email and private retrieval identifiers are not supplied. Its response is dated and request-specific, not a complete funding inventory.

The REA letter is the utility's statement. Its summary and transcription are distinguished from site analysis; the original photographs and PDF remain available. A machine-generated meeting transcript is not signed minutes or an independently certified quotation.

Questions still open

Final signed county conditions and owner acceptance, the project's state permitting route, reconciliation of the water descriptions, utility tariff applicability and signed agreements, and actual funding/security remain unresolved where identified in the source notes. No absence or misconduct conclusion follows merely from a missing record.

Map locations and lines are approximate or schematic, not verified boundaries, utility routes or infrastructure connections. Consult the map's source notes.
